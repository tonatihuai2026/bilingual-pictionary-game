How to Play · Cómo jugar

  1. Set up 2–4 teams and pick a complexity level (it controls the tile categories).
  2. Each round shows one word in English and Spanish at once. Every team draws and guesses at the same time — it's all-play.
  3. Start the timer. When a team guesses correctly (in either language), tap that team's button.
  4. That team rolls the die and their token advances across the board.
  5. First team to reach the finish tile wins! · ¡El primer equipo en llegar a la meta gana!

Single-device and seeded for reproducible games. Online multiplayer with live drawing is planned for a future version.
