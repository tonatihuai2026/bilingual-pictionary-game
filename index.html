How to Play

  1. Set up your teams (2-4), pick a difficulty level, and a timer length.
  2. Only the current drawer looks at the word (in English and Spanish at once) and draws it on paper or a whiteboard — no talking, no writing letters/numbers.
  3. Roll the dice for a random twist on the round (e.g. draw with your eyes closed, no sounds at all, double points).
  4. Start the timer. Teammates guess out loud in either language.
  5. Mark "Correct" to score a point and move to the next team, or "Skip" if time runs out.

This version is single-device, pass-and-play — everyone gathers around one phone, tablet, or laptop. Online multiplayer with live drawing is planned for a future version.
